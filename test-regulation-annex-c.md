# Annex C — Employee Rights and Data Access Procedures

**Reference:** EDGB/2026/003-C
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-01

---

## C.1 Right to Access

Employees have the right to request a copy of all personal data held by their employer.
Requests must be acknowledged within **5 business days** and fulfilled within **20 calendar days**.

## C.2 Right to Rectification

Employees may request correction of inaccurate personal data at any time.
Employers must action rectification requests within **10 business days**.

## C.3 Right to Erasure

Upon termination of employment, employees may request erasure of personal data not required
for legal retention purposes. Employers must respond within **30 days**.

## C.4 Right to Data Portability

Employees may request their personal data in a machine-readable format (JSON or CSV).
Employers must fulfil portability requests within **20 calendar days**.
